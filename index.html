What the map shows

Each cell compares the same NASA GISS climate model under a high-emissions scenario and a low-emissions scenario. Red means the high-emissions future is warmer; blue means it is cooler. The cells are averages of nearby model grid points so the visualization can load in a browser.

Source: CMIP6 ScenarioMIP, NASA-GISS GISS-E2-1-G, monthly near-surface air temperature.
